## How affiliates get credited

Affiliates are credited for referrals when a user creates an account using a **link or discount code provided by the Affiliate**.

## Public commission tiers

The public Hybrid Funding affiliate page currently lists the following tier structure:

| Tier | Sales volume | Commission |
|---|---:|---:|
| Tier 1 | First 1-9 sales | 5% |
| Tier 2 | 10-49 sales | 10% |
| Tier 3 | 50-100 sales | 15% |
| Tier 4 | 100+ sales | 20% |

The public page also advertises **weekly payouts** and no cap on earnings. Affiliate dashboard terms, the partner agreement, and any later published program terms govern if they differ from this summary.

## How to apply

Apply through the **Affiliate** page on [hybridfunding.co/affiliate](https://www.hybridfunding.co/affiliate).

## Trade House builder model

Affiliates can operate as more than traffic sources. A partner can build a **Trade House**: a trader community organized around education, accountability, market specialization, and competition.

A recommended member path is:

1. Start with the free Trader Playbook.
2. Complete the Trader DNA assessment.
3. Choose a market specialization: Forex, Crypto, Futures, Single Session Equities, or Prediction Markets.
4. Write a risk plan and use the relevant program rules.
5. Participate in education, review sessions, and Trade House Battles.
6. Choose a Hybrid Funding program only if it fits the trader's strategy and risk constraints.
7. Progress from member to contributor, market captain, or Trade House leader.

## Partner marketing standards

Affiliates should:

- Clearly disclose the financial relationship near endorsements and affiliate links.
- Use only current program rules, prices, discounts, payout terms, and platform information.
- Label hypothetical earnings or payout math as examples, not expected results.
- Never promise funding, profitability, payouts, pass rates, or "risk-free" outcomes.
- Describe AI Market Radar signals as **research candidates**, not trade recommendations or guarantees.
- Use commercial email and SMS only in ways that comply with applicable consent, opt-out, advertising, and anti-spam requirements.
- Avoid bought, scraped, or shared contact lists for Hybrid Funding promotions.

## Common questions

**Q:** Can I be both an affiliate and a trader?  
**A:** Yes. The programs are independent. Your trading activity must still comply with the applicable program rules and Prohibited Trading policy.

**Q:** Can I build a community around Hybrid Funding?  
**A:** Yes. Education-first communities and Trade Houses are encouraged as long as the partner does not make unsupported performance claims, coordinate prohibited trading behavior, or misrepresent the affiliate relationship.

**Q:** Where should I get the latest commission and payout terms?  
**A:** Use the affiliate dashboard / partner agreement and the current public Affiliate page. If they conflict, escalate to the affiliate team before publishing a hard claim.

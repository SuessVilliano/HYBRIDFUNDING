## What it is

Prediction Markets is a one-step funded-trading program built around defined real-world event markets. Instead of trading a currency pair, futures contract, cryptocurrency, or equity, the trader takes a **Yes** or **No** position on a specific event outcome.

Each market has a price between $0.00 and $1.00. A winning share settles at **$1.00** and a losing share settles at **$0.00**.

## How a position works

- To take the affirmative side of an outcome, buy **Yes**.
- To take the opposite side, buy **No**. There is no conventional short selling.
- Shares = order amount / contract price.
- Opening trades are allowed only when the contract price is between **$0.20 and $0.80**.
- A position may be closed at other prices after it is open.
- A **1% commission** applies to opening trades.
- There is **no leverage**; the trader pays for the shares purchased.

## Worked example

If a trader buys **$1,000 of Yes at $0.25**, the position holds **4,000 shares** before opening commission.

- If the market price rises to $0.40, the marked value is about $1,600.
- If the trader holds through resolution and the event resolves Yes, winning shares settle at $1.00 each.
- If it resolves No, Yes shares settle at $0.00.

The Max Profit per Event rule still applies to account progression and is intended to prevent one event from dominating account results.

## AI Market Radar

Hybrid Funding's AI Market Radar scans live prediction markets and surfaces mechanically generated **research candidates**.

Current signal classes:

- **MOVER** - large 24-hour probability changes on markets with meaningful volume.
- **VOL SPIKE** - unusually heavy volume relative to book depth without a large price move.
- **DECISION** - markets near resolution that remain materially uncertain.
- **BOOK CHECK** - mutually exclusive outcome books whose bid prices may warrant structural-pricing research.

Radar signals are **not trade recommendations or financial advice** and do not guarantee that a market is mispriced or profitable.

## Common questions

**Q:** Can I short a Prediction Market?  
**A:** No conventional short selling is used. To take the opposite view, buy the other side of the market (for example, buy No instead of shorting Yes).

**Q:** Is there leverage?  
**A:** No. Prediction Market positions are share-based and unlevered.

**Q:** Can I close before resolution?  
**A:** Yes. Positions may be sold before resolution to realize the current gain or loss.

**Q:** Should I trade every signal from AI Market Radar?  
**A:** No. Radar creates a research queue. Traders are responsible for their own research, position sizing, and rule compliance.

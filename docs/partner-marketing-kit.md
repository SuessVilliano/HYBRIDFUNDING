# Hybrid Funding Partner Growth Hub - Content Source

_Last reviewed: 2026-09-20_

This document is the source-of-truth copy library for the Drive-based partner kit.

## Partner positioning

Hybrid Funding should not be promoted as "just another challenge link." Lead with trader education, market access, tools, and fit.

Core value pillars:
- Forex, Crypto, Futures, Single Session Equities, and Prediction Markets
- Trader Playbook + Trader DNA assessment
- AI Market Radar for prediction-market research candidates
- TradeHouse Battles and team-based trader development
- Multiple paths based on market, style, and risk preference
- Affiliate economics for partners who build an audience or TradeHouse

## Recommended partner funnel

1. **Value** - offer the free Trader Playbook or Trader DNA test.
2. **Diagnose** - help the trader identify market/style/program fit.
3. **Demonstrate** - show a platform, Radar workflow, rule example, or Battle.
4. **Convert** - use the partner link/code after value is delivered.
5. **Retain** - invite the trader into a TradeHouse/community or education sequence.
6. **Expand** - introduce additional market access only when it matches the trader.

## Prohibited partner claims

Do not publish:
- guaranteed funding
- guaranteed payout/profitability
- "risk-free"
- "easy money"
- fabricated pass rates, trader counts, payouts, or earnings
- unlabeled hypothetical earnings as expected results
- claims that AI Market Radar predicts outcomes with certainty

# Hybrid Funding Trader Playbook V2

_Last reviewed: 2026-09-20_

## North star

The free playbook should be useful enough that a trader can improve their process without buying anything. Hybrid Funding products should appear as infrastructure underneath the education, not as the reason the education exists.

### Working title

**The Hybrid Funding Trader Operating Playbook**  
Know Your Trader DNA. Choose Your Market. Protect Capital. Build Your Process.

## V1 audit findings

- The generated PDF is 31 pages, while the download page currently advertises 50 pages.
- The PDF metadata shows a May 1, 2026 creation date, before Prediction Markets and AI Market Radar were integrated into the product story.
- The playbook still frames the ecosystem around four asset classes and does not treat Prediction Markets as a fifth market-access path.
- The back third becomes more promotional than educational, including affiliate-income and projected-payout examples that can read like expected results.
- The public affiliate page lists 5%-20% commission tiers and weekly payouts, while the canonical KB says commission structure and payout cadence are only provided after acceptance.
- The affiliate page includes illustrative monthly earnings and referral numbers presented as dashboard statistics without labeling them as examples.
- The repo already contains a Trader DNA assessment, AI Market Radar, Single Session Equities documentation, Trade House Battles, and Prediction Markets educational copy. These should be connected into one learning system.

## V2 design language

Retain the navy/cyan/gold visual system, but make the document feel like an operating manual rather than a sales deck.

Use:
- chapter divider pages
- decision trees
- worksheets
- worked examples
- "RULE / WHY IT EXISTS / HOW TO OPERATE" panels
- "MARKET FIT" cards
- QR/deep links to live tools
- source-date footers on rules pages
- version number + last-updated date

## Claims to remove or rewrite

Remove or substantiate before publication:
- "easy money play"
- projected annual payout examples that can be read as expected results
- "pays for itself" claims
- unsupported "most traders" or "most affiliates" claims
- "only prop firm" claims unless continuously substantiated
- the "50 pages" landing-page claim until the regenerated PDF actually has that page count
- "all four asset classes" language

## Trust standard

A trader should be able to download the playbook, never buy a challenge, and still leave with:
- a clearer trading identity
- a market-fit framework
- a written risk plan
- a sizing method
- a daily checklist
- a weekly review process
- a 30-day practice plan

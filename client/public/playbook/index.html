01 · Identity before product

Start with Trader DNA

The four archetypes are a conversation starter—not a diagnosis and not a guarantee that one program fits you. Use them to identify the behavior you need to control before account size enters the picture.

Sniper

Precision over volume

Natural edge: patience and selectivity. Watch for hesitation or too few reps. Ask: Can I wait without forcing?

Architect

Systems over instinct

Natural edge: repeatability and discipline. Watch for rigidity. Ask: Do I follow tested rules when conditions change?

Hybrid

Adaptability over rigidity

Natural edge: reading changing conditions. Watch for style sprawl. Ask: What stays fixed when I adapt?

Phoenix

Speed + resilience

Natural edge: decisiveness and recovery. Watch for emotional overtrading. Ask: Can I cap aggression?

Prediction Markets: trade the outcome, not the chart

Each market asks a defined question. Buy Yes if you think the outcome happens; buy No if you think it does not. There is no conventional short sale and no leverage.

Worked example

Buy $1,000 of Yes at $0.25 and you hold 4,000 shares before commission. At a $0.40 live price, the marked value is about $1,600. If the event resolves Yes, winning shares settle at $1.00; if it resolves No, Yes shares settle at $0.00. Account-level drawdown and per-event rules still apply.

04 · Risk operating system

Build a process before you build size

A funding account adds constraints. Your job is to know your own constraints first.

Before the session

  • Maximum risk per idea
  • Daily stop
  • Maximum attempts
  • Setup criteria
  • No-trade conditions
  • Relevant event/calendar risk

After the session

  • Rule compliance score
  • Actual R gained/lost
  • Best decision
  • Worst decision
  • Did size change emotionally?
  • One correction for tomorrow

Define

Write the setup and invalidation before entry.

Size

Choose risk from the loss you can accept—not the profit you want.

Execute

Follow the plan and the program's real-time drawdown constraints.

Review

Score process first; P&L second.

05 · 30-Day Trader Build

Practice first. Purchase second.

Week 1 · Observe

Take Trader DNA, choose one market, write setup/no-trade conditions, daily stop, and risk per idea.

Week 2 · Test

Use demo/practice where available. Journal entries, exits, rule mistakes, R, and losing streaks.

Week 3 · Execute

Trade only documented setups with hard daily limits. Review drawdown geometry before each session.

Week 4 · Decide

Review process compliance and choose a program only if its constraints fit the strategy you tested.

A no-purchase decision can be correct. At Day 30, the goal is a written operating system—not a checkout receipt.
